Research Interests

Combining experimental photonics with computational methods to develop precision optical instrumentation and imaging techniques.

Optical Spectroscopy

Photothermal and time-resolved spectroscopic techniques for studying molecular interactions and material properties.

Pump-Probe & Time-Resolved Techniques

Ultrafast laser-based methods for resolving dynamics on femtosecond to nanosecond timescales.

Nonlinear Optics

Characterization of nonlinear optical materials and light-matter interaction phenomena.

Optical Instrumentation Development

Design, build, and integration of custom optical systems for precision measurement and imaging.

Optical Imaging & Microscopy

Confocal, two-photon, FLIM, TIRF, and optical trapping techniques for micro- and nanoscale imaging.

Quantitative Optical Measurements

Signal processing and data analysis pipelines for extracting quantitative information from optical measurements.

Techniques & Instrumentation

Hands-on experience across the full instrumentation stack, from lasers and detectors through to acquisition and analysis software.

Laser Systems

Ultrafast lasers (Coherent Astrella, Coherent MIRA, IMRA) and supercontinuum sources (NKT SuperK Extreme).

Detection Systems

ICCD, CMOS, CCD, PMT, and SPAD detectors; lock-in amplifiers and oscilloscopes.

Automation & Data Acquisition

NI DAQ and LabVIEW-based automation and instrument control for real-time data acquisition.

Computation & Analysis

Python, MATLAB, LabVIEW, COMSOL Multiphysics (Optics Module), Ansys Zemax OpticStudio, OriginPro, and ImageJ.
