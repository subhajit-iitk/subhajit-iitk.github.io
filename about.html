Biography

Dr. Subhajit Chakraborty is a physicist and optical engineer with demonstrated expertise in developing advanced optical spectroscopy and microscopy instrumentation, including ultrafast laser systems, pump-probe platforms, and custom optical setups for precision measurements.

He is skilled in pump-probe, photothermal, FLIM, confocal, two-photon, FCS, and TIRF techniques, with proficiency in optical system design, automation, signal processing, and data analysis using Python, MATLAB, LabVIEW, COMSOL, and ImageJ.

He completed his PhD through a joint program between the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (IIT Kanpur) and The University of Melbourne, working with Prof. Debabrata Goswami and Prof. Trevor A. Smith. Most recently, he worked as a PhD Research Intern at the Samsung R&D Institute India – Bangalore, developing image and video processing techniques to optimize multimedia imaging pipelines.
