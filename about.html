Biography

Dr. Subhajit Chakraborty is a physicist and optical engineer specializing in photonics science and engineering. He completed his PhD through a joint program between the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (IIT Kanpur) and The University of Melbourne, a cross-institutional experience that shaped his interdisciplinary approach to physics and engineering.

His expertise sits at the intersection of optical imaging and precision instrumentation. Combining experimental photonics with computational methods, he builds and refines optical systems for high-resolution microscopy, spectroscopy, and non-destructive optical metrology.

He is increasingly interested in bringing computation closer to the optics themselves: computer vision, automated image analysis, and software-defined imaging systems that adapt to what they observe.
